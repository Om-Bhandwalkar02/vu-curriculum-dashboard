Core Framework

Four Pillars — EEGS

💼
💼 Employability

Projects, industry orientation, AI, ML, IoT, Cloud, Web, mobile development, communication and practical skills. VU shows strongest alignment with employability and industry readiness across all revisions — growing from 30.3% in 2018 to 36.3% in 2024 NEP.

⚖️
⚖️ Ethics & Governance

Research practices, cybersecurity, responsible technology use, and professional communication. Ethics coverage grew from 0% in 2018–2021 to 3.3% in 2023 NEP and 6.3% in 2024 NEP — a significant curriculum maturity improvement.

♀️
♀️ Gender & Inclusion

Current exposure exists mainly through language, societal and interdisciplinary components. An opportunity area — grew from 0% (2018–2019) to 5.0% in 2023 NEP and 3.8% in 2024 NEP. Further integration of inclusion-focused curriculum elements is recommended.

🌿
🌿 Sustainability

Environmental Studies and Green Computing provide a stronger sustainability footprint than many conventional CS curricula. Coverage grew from 0.8% in 2018 to 6.7% in 2023 NEP and 5.0% in 2024 NEP — ahead of the national average for CS programs.
