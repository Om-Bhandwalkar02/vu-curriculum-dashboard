🚀 Entrepreneurship

13 credits · 10.8%

Expanded under NEP — includes Industry Project, Software Project Management, Technology Training of Society, innovation-oriented electives, and entrepreneurship-linked specialization tracks.

Industry Project — 2 cr

Observation Stronger industry and product orientation through project-based learning, community engagement, specialization tracks, and vocational courses. Dedicated entrepreneurship starts after Semester III and can be strengthened through startup incubation exposure.

Includes research methodology, capstone projects, AI/ML, analytics, sustainability-driven inquiry, and project-based learning.

Department Vision

Shaping Future-Ready
Innovators & Leaders

With 51.7% of the curriculum aligned to Entrepreneurship, Research, and Higher Studies pathways, the department fosters an innovation ecosystem that bridges academic rigour with startup readiness, research depth with industry practice, and theoretical foundations with graduate-level ambition.

🚀

Startup Mindset

Project-based learning, industry exposure, and product thinking woven throughout the curriculum.

🔬

Research Excellence

Research begins from Year 1 — capstone projects, methodology, AI/ML, and publication readiness.

🎓

Graduate Readiness

Strong foundation in statistics, AI, analytics, and theory supporting M.Tech/MS/PhD/GATE pathways.
