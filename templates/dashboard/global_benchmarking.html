Key Insights

Benchmarking Findings

🏛️

VU Leads: NEP Compliance, IKS & Multi-Disciplinary Flexibility

Vishwakarma University demonstrates a distinctive advantage in NEP compliance, Indian Knowledge System (IKS) integration, and multi-disciplinary flexibility compared with IISc Bengaluru and BITS Pilani. Through formal integration of NEP-aligned curriculum design, interdisciplinary learning pathways, VSEC, and application-oriented education, VU achieves an estimated score of 90+, vs ~80 for IISc and 84–86 for BITS Pilani. VU stands out for translating NEP 2020 objectives into curriculum practice, positioning it as a strong model for modern, student-centric higher education.

🔬

Research Development Opportunity: Strengthening Research Ecosystem

VU shows strong industry orientation and project-based learning; however, research infrastructure and structured undergraduate research opportunities remain areas for growth. VU is estimated to be 13 points below the national benchmark (IISc + BITS + SPPU) and 28 points below the global benchmark (MIT, Stanford, CMU) in research orientation. While VU already includes Research Methodology and capstone projects, introducing faculty-mentored research, undergraduate publication pathways, and dedicated research seminars could strengthen research excellence and global competitiveness.

⚙️

Hardware & Systems Depth: Opportunity for Strengthening Core Computing

VU demonstrates strong coverage in AI, IoT, Cloud, and applied technologies, but hardware and low-level systems depth remains an area for future enhancement. VU scores approximately 80 in Systems & Infrastructure, compared with 96–97 for MIT, Stanford, and CMU. Global curricula emphasize Computer Architecture, Embedded Systems, OS, hardware labs, and advanced systems design. While VU includes Computer Networks, IoT, IoT Data Acquisition, and Operating Systems, expanding dedicated credits in Computer Architecture, Embedded Systems, hardware-oriented labs, and systems research projects could further strengthen alignment with international computing curricula.

🏭

Industry Readiness: Competitive with BITS Pilani & Ahead in Applied Learning

VU demonstrates strong industry orientation through capstone projects, internships, VSEC-based skill development, industry projects, and application-focused learning. VU's Industry Readiness score (95) is competitive with BITS Pilani (95) and ahead of many traditional university curricula. The next opportunity is to strengthen formal long-term industry partnerships, co-designed curriculum pathways, and outcome-linked internship and placement analytics to further enhance industry impact.

Strategic Vision

VU – LaunchPad to be Global

Vishwakarma University is positioned as a high-industry-readiness, NEP-compliant institution with strong experiential and interdisciplinary foundations. Closing the research and systems gap while maintaining its applied-learning strengths will propel VU into the global top tier.
