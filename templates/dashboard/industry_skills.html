Industry Skill Gaps & Recommendations

Vishwakarma University — 2024 NEP Pattern

📱

Systems, Embedded & IoT — Limited Dedicated Coverage

Some emerging areas still have limited dedicated coverage, particularly Embedded Systems, DevOps, Generative AI, Data Engineering, and advanced product development. To improve industry readiness, the curriculum can introduce focused courses such as:

  • → Embedded Systems & IoT (dedicated)
  • → DevOps & Cloud Engineering
  • → Generative AI Applications
  • → Data Engineering with MLOps
  • → Cybersecurity Labs, Full-Stack Product Development, Research Publication workshops
🧠

AI/ML — Theory Present, Tools Absent

Dedicated training in modern AI engineering tools such as PyTorch, Hugging Face, MLflow, vector databases, and deployment workflows is limited. Industry increasingly expects hands-on experience with end-to-end AI development and MLOps practices alongside theoretical knowledge.

Recommendation

Add MLOps & AI Lab covering model deployment, experiment tracking, and applied AI workflows (PyTorch, Hugging Face, MLflow, vector databases).

☁️

Cloud/DevOps — Cloud Foundations Present, Advanced DevOps Tools Limited

Cloud Computing and cloud-oriented electives build foundational cloud knowledge. However, industry-standard DevOps practices such as Kubernetes orchestration, Terraform infrastructure automation, CI/CD pipelines using GitHub Actions, and observability tools are not explicitly structured. Modern software and cloud roles increasingly expect deployment, monitoring, and automation skills.

Recommendation

Add Cloud DevOps & Kubernetes Lab covering Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, CI/CD, and monitoring tools.

💻

Full-Stack — Strong Software Foundation

Modern frontend frameworks, REST API design, backend integration, and full-stack deployment practices are not explicitly structured as dedicated learning outcomes. Current software roles increasingly expect practical skills in component-based UI development, API-first architecture, and database integration.

Recommendation

Add Advanced Full-Stack Development Lab covering React, REST APIs, backend services, database ORM, and deployment workflows.

Industry Tie-Ups & Programs

🏢

🏢 TCS Initiatives

TCS iON and TCS National Qualifier Test (NQT) are integrated into the curriculum as industry-recognized assessment benchmarks. Students are prepared for TCS placement processes through targeted programming and aptitude skill development aligned with TCS hiring criteria. The curriculum incorporates TCS-relevant skill areas including data structures, algorithms, and software engineering practices.

📜

📜 Certifications & MOOCs

The 2024 NEP curriculum formally integrates SWAYAM/MOOC credit transfer, allowing students to earn credits via NPTEL, Coursera, and other certified platforms. VSEC (Value, Skill, Experiential & Community Learning) courses are certification-linked, enabling students to pursue industry-recognized credentials in Cloud Computing, AI/ML, Cybersecurity, and Full-Stack Development alongside their core curriculum.

🔗

🔗 LinkedIn Learning

Students are encouraged to supplement curriculum learning with LinkedIn Learning paths in technology and professional development. The curriculum's employability skill components — including Business Communication, Professional Writing, and Aptitude & Personality Development — directly align with LinkedIn's top-hiring-role competency frameworks, preparing students for LinkedIn Jobs on the Rise India 2025 roles.

⌨️

⌨️ HackerRank Challenges

Competitive coding and problem-solving skills developed through Problem Solving & Programming, Data Structures, and OOP courses directly align with HackerRank assessment domains. The curriculum's strong programming foundation (Python, Java, C) and algorithmic thinking prepares students for HackerRank skill certifications used by 3,000+ companies globally for technical hiring assessments.
